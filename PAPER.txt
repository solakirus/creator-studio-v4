THE MORNING PAPER — setting it up on nastime
=============================================

Your day as a newspaper, printed every morning before you wake up, so you
can see it without picking up your phone.

  Page A1   the front page: a headline and short story about your day, today's
            list with boxes to tick by pen, this week, the weather, the
            workout, your habit streaks, videos due by channel, anything still
            open from earlier in the week
  Page A2   To film today: every video you're shooting today, each with one
            frame from its shots (A3 if there are more than five)

It runs on nastime beside Creator Studio v4. It reads your plans from Sync Hub
(read only, it never changes anything) and prints on your network printer at
4:30 every morning. Its code comes from GitHub and keeps itself up to date.


WHAT YOU NEED (about 10 minutes)
--------------------------------

1. Your printer's address.
   The printer has to be on your home network (Wi-Fi or a cable). Print its
   network page from the printer's own menu, or look it up in the eero app
   under devices. It looks like 192.168.4.50.
   Tip: in the eero app, reserve that address for the printer so it never
   changes.

2. Your Sync token.
   The same one you typed into the app under Settings › Sync (it's also in
   Sync Hub's settings in CasaOS).

3. Optional: a Claude token, so Claude writes the headline and story.
   This uses your Claude plan. No API key, nothing extra to pay.
   On the laptop, open PowerShell and run:

       claude setup-token

   (Claude Code is already on the laptop for the Planner's Draft. If not:
    irm https://claude.ai/install.ps1 | iex)
   A browser opens; approve it. It prints a long token starting sk-ant-oat01-.
   Copy it. It lasts a year.
   Without it, plain rules write the words and the paper still prints.


PUT IT ON NASTIME
-----------------

4. Open CasaOS (http://192.168.4.23). Open the Creator Studio v4 app's
   settings, choose Import, and paste the whole of nas/docker-compose.yml
   (or App Store › Custom Install › Import). It now has a third part,
   creator-studio-v4-paper. In that part, fill in:

       SYNC_TOKEN: "your sync token"
       PRINTER: "192.168.4.50"                (your printer's address)
       CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN: "sk-ant-oat01-…"   (or leave it empty)

   Everything else can stay. You can change these too:
       PRINT_AT      "04:30"           when it prints (24-hour)
       PRINT_DAYS    "0123456"         0 is Sunday; "12345" for weekdays only
       PAPER_NAME    "The Kris Times"
       MASTHEAD      blackletter or redhat
       DUPLEX        on prints both sides of one sheet, off for two sheets

   Save. The first start takes a few minutes: it installs Chromium (which
   makes the PDF) and Claude Code. After that it starts in seconds.

5. On the laptop, run update.bat once. That copies the new Caddyfile to
   nastime, so the paper also answers on the app's usual address (:8795).
   Until then it answers on its own port, :8796, and the app uses that.


TRY IT
------

6. Open  http://192.168.4.23:8795/paper   (or :8796/paper before step 5).
   - Check the printer: it should name your printer, say "ready", and that
     it prints both sides.
   - The page shows today's paper. Pick a date to see another day.
   - Print it now: it makes the paper and prints it. With Claude this takes
     about a minute.

7. In the app: Settings › Morning paper.
   - Print today's paper — any time, from the phone, laptop or iPad.
   - See the paper — opens the page from step 6.
   - Every morning — says when and where it prints, and how the last one went.


IF SOMETHING'S OFF
------------------

- "Not printed — the printer isn't answering": the printer is off or asleep
  on a deep-sleep setting, or its address changed. It tries again every 15
  minutes until 7:30, then waits for tomorrow.
- The printer answers but nothing comes out, or it says it doesn't take PDFs:
  set PRINTER to raw://192.168.4.50:9100 (the printer's raw port).
- "Sync Hub didn't accept the token": SYNC_TOKEN doesn't match Sync Hub's.
- Words by "rules" instead of Claude: the page's status says why. Usually the
  token is missing or a year old — run claude setup-token again.
- The fonts look plain: nastime couldn't reach Google Fonts the first time.
  Delete the paper/fonts folder in /DATA/AppData/Projects/creator-studio-v4
  and restart the paper.
- Logs: CasaOS › Creator Studio v4 › creator-studio-v4-paper › Logs.

Files on nastime: /DATA/AppData/Projects/creator-studio-v4/paper
  app/    the code (from GitHub)       out/    the last 3 weeks of PDFs
  data/   the read-only copy of your plans    state.json   what printed when
