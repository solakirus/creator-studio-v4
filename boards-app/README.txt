Boards — the canvas that used to live inside Creator Studio
============================================================

Open index.html in Chrome or Edge (double-click it). Keep the "fonts" folder
next to it. Everything you make saves in that browser, on that computer.

Bring your boards over from Creator Studio
  1. In Creator Studio: Settings > Export my boards. It saves a .json file.
  2. Here: the menu (top right) or the side panel > "Import a board or backup",
     and pick that file. Your boards arrive as a board on the canvas.

Moving boards to another computer: "Export all boards" here, then
"Import a board or backup" there.
