# Creator Studio v4 — what changed from Creator Studio 3

Built 2026-11-11. Read `CREATOR-STUDIO-3.md` and `HANDOVER.md` first; everything
there still holds. This file covers only the differences.

## Where the code came from

Two copies had split after 2026-10-20:

## A separate app

Nothing is shared with Creator Studio 3, so both can be installed and the iPad
(where both load from `solakirus.github.io`) can't mix them:

| | CS3 | v4 |
|---|---|---|
| app id (Android + Windows) | com.solakirus.creatorstudio3 | com.solakirus.creatorstudio4 |
| name | Creator Studio 3 | Creator Studio v4 |
| link scheme | cs3:// | cs4:// (widgets, shortcuts, board links, Storyboard back-links) |
| IndexedDB | creator-studio-3 | creator-studio-4 (board.html follows `CS_DB_NAME`) |
| Sync Hub app | creator-studio-3 | creator-studio-4 |
| update site / repo | …/creator-studio-3/ | …/creator-studio-v4/ |
| service-worker cache prefix | cs3- | cs4- (CS3's SW deletes `cs3-*` caches it doesn't own) |
| updater localStorage key | cs3-updater | cs4-updater |

Internal names (`cs3:` IPC channels, `CS3Lib`, `CS3Update`, `cs3lib://`,
`cs3-web` update format) were left alone: they never leave one install.
`app/tests/checks.test.js` fails if any identity line points back at CS3.

## Frames travel with tasks; the Library stays on the laptop

**`app/js/taskframes.js`** (new) and store **`task-frames`** (DB 36, keyPath `uid`).

- `keep()` — on a device with a Library: every Library uid that a *live* task or
  video contains (page spots, extra pages, anything matching `db:li_…` or
  `h:<root>|<path>`) is copied into `task-frames` under that same uid, file
  and (for clips) poster included. Over 30 MB is skipped.
- `MediaLib.load()` puts kept frames into `byUid`/`byPlain` (not into `items`,
  so they never show in the Library's own grids; the Library's copy wins).
  `url`/`posterUrl`/`blobOf` read `src:'tf'` from the store. So `Notebook`,
  task pages and everything else draw them with no other change.
- `sweep()` — on every device: deletes copies nothing live points at.
  Live = task with no day, or task date ≥ today − 21 days; video not
  published, or published < 21 days ago (`Videos.save` stamps `publishedAt`).
  A copy younger than a day is never swept (arrival-order grace).
  Library files are never touched.
- Runs 6 s after start, 4 s after any task/video save, and after a sync that
  brought something in.
- `Notebook.mediaFor` no longer caches "missing", so a frame that arrives later shows.

**Gap 1** — `MediaLib.index()`: a frame copied in by `bringIn` also answers to
its old folder uid (`from`) and path.

**Gap 2** — `ShotTemplates.pageFrom(t)`: a template's shots become a
task-sequence page (≤14 spots, each with the shot's frame and line).
`putOnDay` gives the shoot task that page (shots past 14 stay as plain steps;
the template's notebook pages go on as `morePages`). `newVideo` stores it as
`video.page`; `Videos.schedule` copies it onto the task; the video sheet shows
it as a strip (`TaskFrames.strip`). `QuickAdd.add` accepts `page`, `pageFrom`,
`morePages`.

**Laptop-only** — Content's Library and Templates sections show only when
`window.CS3Lib` exists (Electron). Sync never sends `lib-items`, `lib-blob`,
`lib-poster`, `lib-boards`, `nb-music`, or the `library` setting.
Templates *do* sync (small; the phone's "Change page…" uses them). Sample
templates now have fixed ids and are seeded past sync (`Store.raw`), so a
fresh phone can't push samples over ones edited on the laptop.

## Find links on Instagram, TikTok, X profiles

v4 is a new app, so its grab browser (`persist:cs3-grab`, inside v4's own
userData) starts signed out. Instagram then shows a login wall, yt-dlp can't
list a profile, and the old scanner listed the wall's own links (Log In, Sign
Up, followers) as "videos".

- `grab-browser.js`: `open()` spots a sign-in wall on those sites (login
  address, visible password box, or a sign-in dialog) and shows the window so
  the person signs in once; the saved profile keeps it. `postLinks()` scrolls
  a screen at a time and gathers links as it goes, because the grids drop rows.
  `cookieFile()` writes that sign-in as a cookies.txt.
- `grab-builtin.js`: `postLink()` keeps only posts and reels (Instagram
  `/p/ /reel/ /tv/`, TikTok `/video/ /photo/`, X `/status/`, Facebook reels,
  Threads posts), tidied to the post itself; reels are `video`, posts `post`.
  `scan()` sends social profiles straight to the browser (no yt-dlp). Downloads
  of social posts use the app's sign-in via `--cookies` unless a browser is
  picked in settings, and a photo-only post falls back to taking its pictures.
- Test: `app/tests/electron/grab-social.e2e.js` — a mock Instagram (login wall,
  then a grid that keeps only nearby rows) with a stand-in Electron over
  Chromium. 13 checks; the pre-fix code fails 7 of them with the exact links
  from Kris's screenshot.

## Shoot mode

`app/js/screens/shoot-run.js` (new, screen `shoot-run`, `window.ShootRun`):
one shot at a time, full screen — frame (GIF as `<img>`, clip as a muted
looping `<video>`, still), type, line, note, what's next. Got it ticks the shot
exactly as the task screen does (`TaskPages.match`, the page spot and every
step standing for it; all steps → task done) and jumps to the next one not
taken; ‹ › and swipe move without ticking; tapping the picture hides the
chrome; ← → Space/Enter Esc on the laptop. Screen wake lock while open. A task
with no shot page walks its steps instead. Closing calls `params.onChange`.

Ways in: the orange **Shoot mode** button and the **Next up** bar on the shot
page; tapping a picture spot on the shot page (laptop: a *filled* spot — an
empty one still opens the Library picker, and right-click → Pick a shot changes
a frame; phone: any spot); Today's card chip **▶ shoot n/m**, which replaces
the "page n/m" chip that repeated the steps count (the pages stay under the
expanded steps); and the `shoot` shortcut/widget, which falls back to today's
shot-page task when there's no episode day.

The task's shot page canvas now animates (≈12 fps, only while on screen):
`paintPage(ctx, P, t)` passes `t` to `Notebook.draw`, clips are played muted.

Tests: `tests/browser/v4-shoot.browser.js` (25 checks, phone size).
`taskpage.browser.js` now runs as the laptop and checks the new chip.

## Templates, hold to swap, a big Deck, the NAS copy (Nov 11 night)

**Templates in Add a task** (`taskform.js`). "Shots from a template" lists the
templates with shots. Picking one names the task (if blank), makes it a Shoot
task, and on save builds `page` with `ShotTemplates.pageFrom(tp)`: the

Repeats each get their own deep copy. `TaskFrames.wanted()` now also keeps every
`shot-templates` frame, so a template's frames are on the phone before it is
ever used (a saved template keeps them for good; delete the template and the
21-day rule decides).

**Your own groups.** Starred groups take any name ("Morning coffee", "Morning

**Deck tools for a big library** (`screens/library.js`, `MediaLib.deck`).

**The NAS Shot Library with a copy on this computer.** Any folder in Folders

  (different size, or newer by 2 s+) via `<file>.cs4part` then rename, keeps
  the mtime; skips dot files and top-level `_Deleted`/`_logs`; refuses a copy

- Tests: `app/tests/electron/lib-mirror.test.js` (24, real temp folders, now

## Clips reworked: Purpose, Script, structures, script lines on shots (Nov 12)

**Plan** — References first, then two groups:
- *Purpose*: what it's for, type, who it's for, the promise (+ where it pays
  off) and length, as small chips and boxes. Holding the middle is gone (and
  its readiness gap).
- *Script*: the hook (+ how it works), the **structure**, and every shot under
  its beat with the lines said over it. Call to action stays its own fold.
  An older clip's whole script is kept in a fold; "Paste a whole script"
  splits it (or anything pasted) across the shots at blank lines — `TEXT:` goes
  on screen, `CAM:` is to camera, the rest is voiceover.

**Script lines** live on the step: `sub.lines = [{ k:'vo'|'cam'|'txt', t, hook? }]`
(shotlist.js carries them through `norm`, `toTask`, `fromTask` and every host,
so the clip's/video's/sequence's plain shot list has them too; `copyPage`
carries them onto a shoot task). The hook is shot 1's first line
(`hook:true`): whichever of the box or the line was changed last wins
(`plan.hookT` / `line.at`).

**Structures** (`structures.js`) — seven built in (Open loop, Problem →
solution, Story arc, Countdown list, Before → after, Tutorial, Day in the
life) and your own (`settings/structures`, made in the sheet's editor: beats
with what each is for, a first shot and its type). Picking one sets
`plan.beats` (the flow), `plan.beatGuide`, `plan.structure`; an empty clip
gets a shot per beat, one with shots has them spread across the beats. Each
shot's beat is `sub.beat` (it inherits from the shot before when unset), so
moving or removing shots on the Shoot tab never mixes beats up. Beats can be
renamed, moved, removed and added; a shot moves beat from its menu. Chips at
the top jump between beats. Series videos get the same ("Structure and
script" on the video page; `v.beats`…); the clip's structure shows on its card.

**Steps everywhere** (screens/task.js) — each step has a ▸ Script twirl that
edits its lines in place; the beat names head the steps (`t.beatNames`, lent by
the host or copied onto a shoot task); the step list folds to one line
(`cs4.stepsFolded`, remembered) while the page arrows, the shot page and Next
up stay; the shot page always shows (the phone's "Show the page" is gone).

**Shoot mode** — the shot first; "Script for this shot" reveals its lines big
(stays open between shots and visits; `S` toggles it on a keyboard); the beat
name sits in the shot's kicker.

**Shoot tab** — the day row, Steps + Shot page, Notes, References. Capture for
N versions, the Flow and the Script are gone from here.

**Edit tab** — the Cut list (every shot in story order under its beat: frame,
taken or not — from the shoot task's ticks — the words over that cut, and an
edit note `sub.editNote`), Versions ticked as each is cut, Notes. No whole
script, length or references.

**Post tab** — "Where each version goes" (the old Cuts list merged into
Versions: platforms and a link per version, ticked as posted), Title and
thumbnail, Caption + tags + search words, What counts as working. Cuts and
Where it is are gone.

Phone fit (same day): the lines took a narrow column beside the frame and
buttons and wrapped a few words a line. Now a step's Script opens under the
row at the list's full width; on the Plan's script a phone drops the frame
column (the shot number sits by its name); the line's kind is a small chip
you tap through VO → CAM → TXT instead of a menu; the beat menu sits beside
"+ Add a line". The view's classes are `sx-` (`st-` was taken in styles.css).

Test: `app/tests/browser/v4-script.browser.js` (55 checks, phone widths included).

## Shoot mode across pages, and on to the next task (Nov 12)

A task (or a clip, video or sequence through its host) can have more than one
page of shots: the shot page and the pages kept beside it (`morePages`).
Shoot mode now covers all of them (`ShootRun.pagesOf`, `shotsOf(t, k)`,
`applyDone/applyFrame(t, i, v, k)`; page k > 0 ticks its own spots, as the
task screen does):
- "‹ Page 1 of 2 · name ›" under the dots; › past a page's last shot goes on
  to the next page, ‹ before its first comes back to the last; `[` `]` or
  PgUp/PgDn on a keyboard. On a page's last shot "Up next" says where › goes.
- A page finished: **Next page →** (the next page with shots left, opened on
  its first one not taken), then **Next shoot task →**, Back, Look through
  them again. Everything finished: Next shoot task first.
- **⏭ Next task** in the top bar at any time: the next of today's shoot tasks
  with shots left (by time, after this one), opened in place of this one
  (`Screens.replace`), so Back still lands where you started.
- From the task screen, Shoot mode (and a frame's tap, and Next up) opens on
  the page that is showing, and is offered on every page, not just the first.

## Series pages: scrolling, one list of shots (Nov 12)

**Scrolling in Content.** Series, Clips and Episodes didn't scroll under a
finger when the swipe started on the list. `#ctWrap` had `overflow-x:hidden`
(which makes overflow-y `auto`, a scroll box of its own), and a section that is
a `.view` (Series sets that class on the wrapper; embedded screens bring one)
added `overscroll-behavior:contain` — a box with nothing to scroll that won't
pass the swipe on to `#view`. Now `#ctWrap`, `#ctWrap.view` and `#ctWrap > .view`
are `overflow-y:visible; overscroll-behavior:auto` with `overflow-x:clip`.
Checked with raw touch events (CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`; Chrome's
synthesized scroll gesture doesn't scroll anything headless): before, 0 px on
all three; after, they scroll both ways.

**Back to the top** (app.js `wireToTop`): a ↑ button shows once you're about a
screen down whatever is on top (a tab or a pushed screen); a tap on a screen's
title or a tab's page title does the same.

**One list of shots on a series video.** (Superseded the same day — series
videos are clips now, see below; the bar lives on a clip's Shoot tab.) The page
listed the shots twice (the structure's shot-by-shot list and the Steps). The Steps stay — frames, ticks,
the shot page, shoot mode, beat headings, each shot's Script — and the
structure is a bar over them (`Structures.bar`): Change / Pick, a chip per beat
that jumps to its heading in the Steps (`.tk-beat[data-beatk]`), **Edit beats**
(rename, move, remove, add; shots follow their beat, a removed beat's go to
the one before) and **Paste a script**. A shot moves to another beat from the
menu at the foot of its Script panel (task.js `.tk-lbeat`, wherever a record
